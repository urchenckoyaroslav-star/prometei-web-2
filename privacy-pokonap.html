PokoNap Privacy Policy

Provisional privacy information for PokoNap, an educational Android game project in development.

Restoration draft · review required — this policy is prepared from available product information. The current production release, SDKs and Google Play Data safety declarations must be verified before this page is used as an official legal replacement.

Development status

PokoNap is an independent educational Android game project. Release and closed-testing settings can change during development. This page is not evidence of an approved public Google Play release.

Game data

The game may store local progress, preferences and state. Any network, Bluetooth, advertising, analytics or purchase functionality must be checked against the actual build before publication. Do not assume a feature is active merely because it is contemplated here.

Children and advertising

If PokoNap targets children, its advertising, data practices, consent handling and age-targeting controls must meet relevant Google Play Families requirements before release. The developer has not verified those settings in this document.

Control and questions

You can clear locally stored data through Android app settings, subject to device backup behavior. Check the official Google Play listing once available for the active support and privacy details.
