05 / DISCOVER PROMETEI RADIOONLINE RADIO AND MUSIC

MUSIC IN
THE PROMETEI UNIVERSE.

PROMETEI FM is a music page by the team behind PROMETEI Messenger. Our DJs choose the collections; visitors listen and send song requests.

How do I tune in?

Press LISTEN on the player. PROMETEI FM comes alive when the station is broadcasting.

Can I listen on my phone?

Yes. Tune in from Android, iPhone, tablet or desktop. Your browser may ask you to tap Play.

Can I request a song?

Yes. Send us an artist, title and optional dedication. Requests are free and open your email application.

Where can I discover more music?

Explore the chart, listen to the selection and vote for the tracks you love.
