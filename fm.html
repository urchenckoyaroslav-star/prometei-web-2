05 / DISCOVER PROMETEI RADIOONLINE RADIO AND MUSIC

MUSIC IN
THE PROMETEI UNIVERSE.

PROMETEI FM is a music page by the team behind PROMETEI Messenger. Our DJs choose the collections; visitors listen and send song requests.

How does PROMETEI FM work?

Listen to curated YouTube playlists in the official visible embedded player. Videos may contain ads and vary by region. The embedded player is a separate session for each visitor, not synchronized live radio.

Can I listen on my phone?

Yes. The page adapts to Android, iPhone, tablets and desktops. Press LISTEN to begin; browsers may require a tap before playing audio.

Can I request a song?

Yes. Enter the artist, title and optional dedication in the request form. It prepares a free email message to our team. Paid orders are not accepted.

How is music broadcast from a PC?

Our DJ can switch the station to an authorized MP3/AAC stream served over HTTPS. The public stream must be set up separately, and music must be licensed for broadcasting.
