Child Safety Standards

Our published expectations for safe behavior and reporting concerns involving minors.

Zero tolerance for abuse

PROMETEI prohibits child sexual abuse material (CSAM), sexual exploitation or grooming of minors, and any activity that endangers children. We do not knowingly endorse or permit the use of the service for these purposes.

Reporting a concern

If you encounter suspected child sexual abuse material or exploitation, report it promptly to your local law-enforcement or child-protection authority. You can also contact the PROMETEI developer using the official Google Play App support contact.

Do not share or forward suspected illegal images or videos as part of a support request. Provide only essential information such as where the concern was observed and non-graphic context.

Action and cooperation

The developer intends to review actionable abuse reports and, where supported by current app infrastructure, take appropriate account or content actions. Valid requests from authorized authorities may be addressed as required by applicable law.

PROMETEI has multiple network transport modes; not all communications necessarily pass through a centrally moderated service. Users should understand this limitation when reporting or seeking assistance.

For parents and guardians

Discuss digital safety with minors, use device-level parental controls where appropriate, and report immediate risk to local authorities. The current Play Console target-audience declaration must be reviewed before publishing any child-directed claims.
