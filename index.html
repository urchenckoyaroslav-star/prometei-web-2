06 / QUESTIONSGOOD TO KNOW

THE DETAILS
MATTER.

Does PROMETEI always work without Internet?＋

No. Local communication requires compatible devices, suitable radio permissions and an available route. Internet-based messaging still needs connectivity.

Do I need to provide a phone number?＋

The app is designed to start without a phone number or email address. Recovery options may differ from conventional phone-based messengers.

Is PHANTOM GPS available to everyone?＋

Not yet. PHANTOM GPS is in Google Play closed testing. Eligible testers can join the group and opt in before installing.

Where can I read the privacy policy?＋

See the PROMETEI privacy policy and our account-deletion information here.
