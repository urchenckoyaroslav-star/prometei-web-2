How PROMETEI finds a connection

Internet, Bluetooth and local Wi-Fi/LAN take different paths. Here is what each option means in real-world Android use.

Bluetooth: direct communication nearby

PROMETEI explores direct Bluetooth connections when compatible Android phones are close enough. Whether it can connect depends on hardware, Android permissions, Bluetooth availability, signal conditions, and the app's current supported modes. A Bluetooth link does not, by itself, require cellular data or Internet access.

Bluetooth is short-range: walls, radio interference and device power policies may reduce reliability. Both devices need to be able to discover and communicate with one another.

Local Wi-Fi/LAN: on the same network, even without an Internet uplink

A Wi-Fi network can exist without external Internet. When the involved phones can reach one another on that local network, PROMETEI may use the LAN path. For example, devices connected to a local wireless router may communicate on the same local network even if that router has no Internet connection.

The devices must have a compatible reachable route. Guest-network isolation, firewall rules, incompatible devices, Android restrictions or different subnets may prevent local communication. LAN is not the same as a long-distance offline mesh.

Internet: a route beyond the local network

With working Internet access, the app can use supported online message routes. This can connect users who are not physically near each other, subject to service availability and the installed app version.

Internet messaging requires a working data connection; it cannot automatically continue when every possible route is unavailable.

Mixed mode: several paths, one conversation

Mixed mode explores available supported transports — Bluetooth, Wi-Fi LAN and Internet — and may use alternative paths based on the current situation. It does not guarantee a working route when the network or nearby compatible devices are unavailable.

Delivery times and the exact relay behaviour vary by device, connection type and release. We test these cases with real Android devices and continue to improve reliability.

What "offline messenger" means — and what it does not

Offline messaging here means that supported local connections may work without Internet. It does not mean universal communication between any two phones anywhere. Range, permissions, devices, routing and operating system settings still matter.

PROMETEI is not an emergency communication service and should not be relied upon for safety-critical alerts. Connection methods may expose different technical metadata; review the privacy information for details.
