Website privacy and advertising

This page describes the PROMETEI FM website and its music chart.

Chart votes

The voting service derives a weekly identifier from your IP address to limit repeat votes from the same connection. The vote records and totals expire after eight days. The published chart shows aggregate vote shares. Website delivery and requests are handled by Cloudflare.

YouTube and other media sites

Starting the chart player loads a YouTube embed. YouTube can receive your IP address and browser information when the player loads. Opening a video or source link takes you to the publisher’s website and its privacy policy.

Google advertising

On chart pages that show Google AdSense, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or device identifiers to deliver and measure ads. If permitted by your choices, Google and its partners may use visits to this and other websites to personalize ads.

See how Google uses data on partner websites and manage personalized advertising in My Ad Center. Where Google displays privacy and cookie settings on the chart, you can reopen them to change or withdraw your advertising consent.
