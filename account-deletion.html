Account & Data Deletion

Information for requesting deletion of a PROMETEI account and its associated data.

Restoration draft · review required — this policy is prepared from available product information. The current production release, SDKs and Google Play Data safety declarations must be verified before this page is used as an official legal replacement.

Delete from inside PROMETEI

Open PROMETEI, go to your profile or account settings, and use the account-deletion control provided by your installed release. Follow the confirmation prompts and make any needed backups before confirming.

If the menu is not available in your version, contact the developer via the official Google Play listing and request account and associated data deletion. Include the app name and only the identifier needed to locate the account.

Request outside the app

If you can no longer access the app, open the official PROMETEI Google Play listing, expand App support, and contact the listed developer through that channel with the subject “PROMETEI account deletion request.”

The developer must verify that this off-app request route is operational and update Play Console with a direct accessible deletion-request URL before relying on this page for compliance. Do not include your password, recovery phrase or message contents.

What is removed and what may remain

The expected deletion scope includes any account record and associated service-side personal data held by the developer, subject to applicable law. Other people may retain copies of messages they received, and locally saved content on other devices is not necessarily removed by deleting one account.

Where records must be retained for fraud prevention, legal obligations, or transaction accounting, the developer should disclose the precise categories and periods after checking the current backend.

Questions

Read the Privacy Policy for categories of information potentially processed. If you cannot find the app support contact, check the current official Google Play listing or the Support page on this site.
