About this notice

This notice describes the PROMETEI Android Messenger, package com.meshnetwork.app, and the related website. It is intended to help users understand what data may be involved with each feature.

This document is a restored draft. The developer must verify its statements against the current release and the Google Play Data safety declaration before substituting it as the authoritative privacy policy.

Data and content

When you use PROMETEI, the app may process the display name, local user identifier, profile picture, messages, group or community participation, and any files you choose to share. The app may keep certain data on your device and communicate data to recipients or supporting services, depending on the chosen transport.

Do not enter information you are not comfortable sharing with your intended recipients. Account identifiers and message transport metadata may be needed for delivery and troubleshooting.

Network access and Android permissions

PROMETEI explores Bluetooth, local Wi‑Fi/LAN, Internet, and Mixed message paths. To discover and connect nearby devices, Android may require Bluetooth, nearby-device, local-network, or location-related permissions depending on OS version. A permission request by itself does not mean GPS coordinates are shared.

Nearby devices, routers and internet service providers can observe some technical connection metadata. Actual paths, retries and storage vary with the current version and device conditions.

Third-party services and monetization

Where enabled in your installed version, Google Play Billing may process purchases and Google AdMob may provide advertising. These third parties may process payment, advertising, device or diagnostic data under their own policies. Consult the in-app disclosures and Google Play Data safety section for the release you use.

This website loads web fonts from Google Fonts. It does not use analytics or advertising trackers in its own static code.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored app data may be cleared through the available in-app controls or device settings. Deletion of an account and associated service-side records, where applicable, requires the product’s deletion process. Read the Account Deletion page for steps and available contact routes.

Some information may need to be retained where legally required, including anti-abuse, accounting or purchase records; exact retention periods must be confirmed with the running service.

Security and limitations

We aim to improve reliability and security, but no transmission method is guaranteed to work in every situation. PROMETEI should not be relied on as an emergency communications system. Do not assume that every connection mode provides identical privacy or security guarantees.

Children and contact

See the Child Safety page for safety commitments and reporting guidance. For questions about the application or your data, use the support contact provided by the developer in the official Google Play listing. Never send passwords or private message contents in a support request.
